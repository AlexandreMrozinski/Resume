### **PhD Student in Microbiology**

PhD student investigating the microbiological risks associated with the reuse of treated wastewater for agricultural irrigation and their environmental impacts within a One Health framework. Research focuses on *Clostridioides difficile* and *Clostridium perfringens*, including their prevalence, environmental persistence, toxigenicity, and antimicrobial resistance.
